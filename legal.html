Allergen notice

Please tell us about any food allergy before you order, every time, including when you order online or through a delivery app.

Our kitchen prepares food that contains or may contain these major allergens:

  • Milk (mozzarella, catupiry and other cheeses, table cream, condensed milk, dulce de leche, white chocolate)
  • Wheat and gluten (all pizza crusts, calzones, garlic bread and pepperoni bread)
  • Eggs (House style and Portuguese pizzas)
  • Peanuts (Brazilian peanut candy pizza)
  • Tree nuts (Nutella, Ferrero and other chocolate toppings, and the chopped nut mix)
  • Shellfish (Shrimp supreme pizza)
  • Soy may be present in chocolate toppings and candies.
  • Other allergens may be used in sauces and toppings. Ask us about any specific dish.

All our food is made in one shared kitchen, using shared ovens and equipment. Because of this, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free of any allergen.
