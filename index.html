A little Brazil in Marietta

Brazil Pizza brings the authentic flavors of Brazil to Marietta. Every recipe comes from real pizzerias back home, and we bake with passion and a little bit of Brazilian soul.

Dine in, take out or get it delivered. Our dining room is open every day except Monday.
